OUR STORY

You'll be sure to have an old fashioned time.

There is a certain magic in an old-fashioned barbershop. The steady rhythm of scissors, the scent of warm leather, and the quiet confidence of a perfectly finished cut.

Vellum Grooming was born from a simple belief: exceptional grooming should never feel rushed. We preserve the rituals of traditional barbering while bringing them into the modern age.

Alexander Hayes Founder & Creative Director
